## The Schwarz Cycle Structure

The diagram shows a cyclical pattern of paradigm evolution from ~1500 to 2000+:

### Phases of the Cycle

1. **RENAISSANCE** (1500) - Copernicus, Galileo, Kepler
2. **BAROCCO PERIOD** (1600) - Descartes, Leibniz, Newton
3. **CLASSICAL PERIOD** / "ENLIGHTENMENT" (1700-1800) - Laplace, Kant, A. Comte
4. **STABILITY** (1800) - Maxwell, Marx, Nietzsche
5. **SOCIAL REVOLUTIONS** (1900) - Relativity, Quantum Mechanics
6. **"MODERN" ERA** - Taylorism, Fordism, Consumer Society
7. **"POST-MODERN" ERA** (2000) - Internet, Globalization, Complex Adaptive Systems
